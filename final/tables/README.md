# tables/ — Supplementary Tables + supporting CSVs

Each CSV here corresponds to a numbered Supplementary Table in the
Supplementary Appendix (the manuscript documents are not part of this code
release). The `_supporting/` sub-folder holds intermediate tables that drive the
**main-text figures** but are not themselves printed as Supplementary
Tables.

## Supplementary Tables (S1 – S6)

| # | File | What it holds | Schema | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Table S1** | `TableS1_dengue_source.csv` | Annual Vietnam dengue cases 1990–2025 with the single labelled source per year (OpenDengue / MoH). | `year, cases, source_label, source_tier, aggregation, source, n, unit` | Output of `01_load_opendengue.py` after the fixed-priority merge rule (Supplement §S1.1). |
| **Table S2** | `TableS2_raw_correlations.csv` | Raw Spearman ρ between annual cases and each of the 16 candidate climate predictors at the better-performing of lag 0 / lag 1. p-values and BH-FDR-adjusted p-values included. | `predictor, season, lag, n, rho, p, p_BH, sig5` | Output of `10_fig3_raw_correlations.py`. |
| **Table S3** | `TableS3_trend_correction_comparison.csv` | Spearman ρ between dengue and each predictor under four configurations: raw, linear-detrended, first-differenced, SDI-residualised. p and p_BH for each. | `index_name, season, lag, n, rho_raw, rho_lineardetrend, rho_firstdiff, rho_sdi_residual, rho_*_p, rho_*_pBH` | Output of `05_sdi_residualise.py`. |
| **Table S4** | `TableS4_ar1_prewhitened.csv` | AR(1)-prewhitened Spearman ρ for the two predictors that survived first-differencing, with 95 % paired-bootstrap CI. | `predictor, season, lag, n, rho, ci_lo, ci_hi` | Output of `07_ar_prewhiten.py`. |
| **Table S5** | `TableS5_validation_by_source.csv` | OpenDengue / MoH reported cases vs GBD modelled incidence, **grouped by source block** (mean and median ratio, Spearman ρ over the block). | `source_label, n_years, year_min, year_max, mean_ratio, median_ratio, spearman` | Output of `03_validate_opendengue_vs_gbd.py`. |
| **Table S6** | `TableS6_age_shift.csv` | Mean modelled age of dengue cases and share of cases in 4 age bands (<15, 15–29, 30–49, 50+) per year 1990–2023 (the docx prints decadal snapshots; the CSV holds the full annual series). | `year, total_cases, mean_age, share_<15, share_15-29, share_30-49, share_50+` | Output of `15_figS5_age_distribution.py` (this material moved from main-text Fig 5 to Fig S5 during review). |
